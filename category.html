CATEGORIES OF GAMES:-


  • SURVIVAL HORROR

    • Survival horror focuses on resource scarcity, inventory management, and player vulnerability. Players often have limited ammunition and health, forcing them to choose between fighting or running away.

    Ex:-Resident Evil and Silent Hill

    Resident Evil

    Silent Hill

  • PSYCHOLOGICAL HORROR

    • Psychological horror targets the mind, using unreliable narrators, mental instability, guilt, and surreal imagery rather than sudden jump scares. These games mess with the player's perception of reality.

    Ex:-Amnesia:The Dark Descent and Layers of Fear

    Amnesia:The Dark Descent

    Layers of Fear

  • JUMPSCARE HORROR

    • These games rely heavily on sudden visual and auditory shocks to startle the player, often set within a contained, fixed environment where managing systems or tracking movements is crucial.

    Ex:-Five Nights at Freddy's and Don't Scream

    Five Nights at Freddy's

    Don't Scream

  • ACTION HORROR

    • Action horror blends traditional horror themes and monsters with fast-paced combat and shooter mechanics. Players are well-equipped to fight hordes of enemies, shifting the focus from pure survival to survival through firepower.

    Ex:-Dead Space and Left 4 Dead

    Dead Space

    Left 4 Dead
